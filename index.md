# Hello World
![Me on Cerro Santa Lucía in Santiago de Chile.](/images/cerro.jpg){:style="float: left; margin-right: 15px; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="200px"}
My name is Niklas Rieken and I am a computer scientist from Dortmund, Germany.

I studied computer science at [RWTH Aachen University](https://rwth-aachen.de) and obtained my master's degree in 2019.
In 2025, I also obtained my doctorate degree in economics after being a PhD student at the [Chair of Management Science](https://www.oms.rwth-aachen.de).
My field of research was combinatorial optimization, in particular matroids, in the broader context of auction theory.
However, as a computer science graduate, I also like formal language theory, logic, and automata theory.
And completely out of the ivory tower into the real world, I am very passionate about automation, digitalization, and optimization of tedious and repetitive processes.

Apart from that I am sports enthusiast, especially football (American and European), baseball, ultimate frisbee, table tennis, and chess.
I also enjoy listening to and playing (guitar and ukulele) music and watching movies.
