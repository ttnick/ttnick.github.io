Currently I am working on three software projects:

 * [`jobscanner`](https://gitlab.com/ttnick/jobscanner) A Telegram bot written in Python that scrapes your favorite companies' websites for new job listings and notifies you when a new role is posted. WIP. (see [blog post](/blog/2026/05/19/introducing-jobscanner))
 * [`HeHu`](https://gitlab.com/ttnick/hehu) A CRUD app based on Flask for head hunters and personnel consultings agencies. WIP.
 * [`ilk`](https://gitlab.com/ttnick/ilk) A REPL for matroid computations, written in C. WIP.

 In the past, I have been also involved in

  * [`GCG` (*generic column generation*)](https://gcg.or.rwth-aachen.de/) A MIP solver based on [`SCIP`](https://www.scipopt.org/) using column generation written in C and C++. My contribution however, was rather focused on Python, bash, and awk scripts that were supplemental to the project. The next project was a byproduct of that work.
  * [`strIPlib` (*structured Integer Program library*)](https://striplib.or.rwth-aachen.de) A library containing integer programs that are somewhat structured (i.e. they have a good Dantzig-Wolfe decomposition) for testing, benchmarking, and experimenting. Initial test runs of `GCG` with extraction of statistics and most of the documentation were done by me.
