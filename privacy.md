I do not collect any data at all.
This page sets exactly one cookie and this one has the only purpose to keep track whether you already have seen the cookie banner in the past 14 days or not.

Cookie banners are one of the monuments for why Europe is losing in tech [as](https://world.hey.com/dhh/cookie-banners-show-everything-that-s-wrong-with-the-eu-3bfcf03d) [DHH](https://youtu.be/UMLgenmD2aY) [states](https://x.com/dhh/status/1970159643495150064).
There hardly is a problem with cookies at all from a privacy point of view.
Of course, I get that targeted ads are a concern for people but this never should have amounted to this eye cancer of a solution.
It is (and always was) an issue (if you really want to call it that) which had a much simple and flexible client-side solution.
